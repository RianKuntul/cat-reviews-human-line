One funny single-line one-star review from a house cat about its human, for
the make.fun competition "Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human".

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the review to `/output/entry.txt` — one line,
under 200 characters, in the deadpan shape of a real one-star review:

> One star. He waits until my bowl shows bottom before refilling it, like I
> eat on a schedule, then sits in my chair and acts surprised when I sit on
> his keyboard mid-call.
